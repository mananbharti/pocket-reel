See it first

The exact picture, before you convert.

Pocket Reel shows the output size, frame rate, a real preview frame and how big the file will be. For classic players the preview is pixel for pixel what the player will show.

Exactly right

160 × 120. 21.68 frames per second. Byte for byte.

The original converter was taken apart to the last header field. Pocket Reel writes the very same MJPEG and IMA ADPCM AVI, so old players simply work.

Features

Everything the original did.
And everything it didn’t.

  • Batch and queue

    Convert many files in one go, one at a time or up to four at once. Cancel or retry any file.

  • Real progress

    Percent, speed and a calm time-remaining estimate for every file and the whole queue.

  • Preview and size

    See the output frame and size before you start. Estimates come from your actual video.

  • Trim

    Keep only the part you want, with sliders or typed times down to a tenth of a second.

  • Extract audio

    Pull the soundtrack out as MP3, AAC, FLAC or WAV, at the quality you choose.

  • Your settings

    Any width and height, four ways to frame the picture, frame rate, video and audio bitrate.

Design

Calm, clear and quick.

Classic players

The format your player was built for.

Small MP3/MP4 players with Actions chips only play one very particular kind of AVI. Pocket Reel reproduces it field for field, including the reserved header values the player firmware reads, and its headers hash identically to the original encoder’s.

Read the reverse-engineering notes →
Container
AVI with idx1 index, 4-byte aligned chunks
Video
MJPEG, YCbCr 4:2:0, quality-75 tables
Audio
IMA ADPCM, 22,050 Hz, mono
Frame rate
21.68 or 14.42 fps, one audio block per frame
Sizes
96×64 up to 320×240, twelve in all, or your own
Picture
Stretch like the original, or fit, fill, keep shape

Accessibility

Made for everyone. Really.

  • Screen readers hear every control, progress as it changes, and when files finish.
  • Keyboard reaches everything: Ctrl O to add, Ctrl Enter to convert, arrows to move.
  • Auto, Light and Dark themes, plus an increase-contrast mode.
  • Reduce motion swaps movement for gentle fades, and follows your system.
  • Big targets everywhere, larger still on touch screens.
  • Private: no accounts, no tracking, no network. Files stay on your device.
