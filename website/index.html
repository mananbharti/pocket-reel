Step 1

Add your videos.

Choose them or drop them in. One or a hundred: MP4, MKV, MOV, phone videos, anything.

Step 2

Press Convert.

Each video shows its size first, then a progress bar and the time left. When it says “Ready for your player”, it is.

Step 3

Copy it to your player. It just plays.

Pocket Reel writes exactly what the official converter writes: 160 × 120, 21.68 frames per second, byte for byte. Save straight to the player if you like.

Design

Simple enough for anyone.

Classic players

The format your player was built for.

Audiocular and other small MP3/MP4 players built on Actions chips only play one very particular kind of AVI. Pocket Reel reproduces it field for field, including the reserved header values the player firmware reads, and its headers hash identically to the original encoder’s.

Read the reverse-engineering notes →
Container
AVI with idx1 index, 4-byte aligned chunks
Video
MJPEG, YCbCr 4:2:0, quality-75 tables
Audio
IMA ADPCM, 22,050 Hz, mono
Frame rate
21.68 or 14.42 fps, one audio block per frame
Screen
160×120 by default, eleven other sizes from 96×64 to 320×240
Picture
Fills the whole screen, like the official tool

Accessibility

Made for everyone. Really.

  • Screen readers hear every control, progress as it changes, and when files finish.
  • Keyboard reaches everything: Ctrl O to add, Ctrl Enter to convert, arrows to move.
  • Light and dark follow your system, and so does high contrast.
  • Reduce motion swaps movement for gentle fades, and follows your system.
  • Big targets everywhere, larger still on touch screens.
  • Private: no accounts, no tracking, no network. Files stay on your device.
